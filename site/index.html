Snap Studio

Prepare your Snapmaker prints, simply

The desktop workshop for your Snapmaker U1: from the file to the print, in one free app.

Download View on GitHub

What it does

🖨

Made for the Snapmaker U1

The U1 plate, profiles and four heads are built in. Printers are found on your network. Status and sending also work with any Klipper (Moonraker) printer.

✂️

Orca-style slicing

The Snapmaker Orca engine is downloaded once and kept up to date: no need to install Orca. Quality, infill and walls, plus advanced settings for supports, brim, seam and multi-material, which you can save as your own profiles.

🎨

Multi-color, head by head

The spools loaded on the printer are read. Each color of a model, painted ones included, goes to the head you pick, and the 3D view shows the real spool colors.

📡

Live printer monitoring

Progress, layers, temperatures of the four heads and the plate, the live camera, pause and cancel, and a print queue. A maintenance view tracks what needs servicing.

✦

AI assistant

Describe a change or a new part: Claude models it through Claude Code with your own account, with a live 3D preview and every version kept. The results are multicolor 3MF files ready to slice.

📁

Your files and model sites

Thumbnails of your 3MF files (Orca, Bambu, Prusa, MakerWorld) and layer-by-layer G-code previews. MakerWorld, Printables, Thingiverse and Cults3D open in tabs, and downloads land in Files.

System requirements

  • macOS on Intel or Apple Silicon (one universal app).
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64), with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime. The installer sets it up if it's missing.
  • Linux x86-64 (AppImage or .deb). ARM64 builds exist too, but without the slicing engine yet.
  • An internet connection the first time, to download the slicing engine (about 30 MB).
  • A Snapmaker U1 on your local network. Any Klipper (Moonraker) printer works for status and sending.

The packages aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. On first launch on macOS, right-click the app and choose Open; on Windows, click "More info" → "Run anyway".
