About

    

      
      Snap Studio
    

    
Snap Studio is free software, developed in the open on GitHub. A bug, an idea, a printer that misbehaves? Say so: that is what moves the app forward.

    

        
        
        
        
        
    

    
Before reporting, check whether the subject is already among the open issues. 

    
Free software under the GNU GPL v3. Slicing engine: Snapmaker Orca (AGPL-3.0); libraries: marked, DOMPurify, xterm.js.
